Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Who we are

Eira ("we", "us") is an independent journal about self discovery, life and beauty. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the site and how we handle it.

What we collect

  • Email address — only if you subscribe to the newsletter. We use it solely to send the weekly email. We never sell or share it.

Cookies

The public site does not set tracking cookies. A session cookie is used only for the site administrator's login.

How long we keep data

Newsletter email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your email address or any data we hold about you at any time. Contact us via the contact page and we'll action it promptly. If you're in the UK/EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Changes

We'll update this page if our data practices change, and update the date at the top.
