01 A note from Eira

Holla I’m Eira Ab-out me

I’ve always wanted more for myself more experiences, more confidence, more knowledge, more freedom, and a life that genuinely feels like mine. But wanting more doesn’t mean I’ve always known exactly where I’m going. I overthink. I compare myself sometimes. I question whether I’m doing enough. I change my mind. I start again.

What I’ve learned is that becoming the woman you want to be isn’t about suddenly having everything figured out. It’s about paying attention to yourself, trying things, getting things wrong, learning what matters to you, and slowly becoming more intentional about the life you’re building.
I’m still doing exactly that.
And if you are too, we probably have more in common than you think.
