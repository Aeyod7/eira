Holla I’m Eira Ab-out me

I’ve always wanted more for myself more experiences, more confidence, more knowledge, more freedom, and a life that genuinely feels like mine. But wanting more doesn’t mean I’ve always known exactly where I’m going. I overthink. I compare myself sometimes. I question whether I’m doing enough. I change my mind. I start again.

What I’ve learned is that becoming the woman you want to be isn’t about suddenly having everything figured out. It’s about paying attention to yourself, trying things, getting things wrong, learning what matters to you, and slowly becoming more intentional about the life you’re building.
I’m still doing exactly that.
And if you are too, we probably have more in common than you think.

NOTES WE LIVE BY

Pay attention to the distance between who you are and who you want to become. That gap does not have to be used against yourself. It can reveal what you value, what you want and where you want to grow. This idea closely connects with research on discrepancies between our current and ideal selves.

You don’t need to have yourself figured out to trust yourself. Clarity develops as you live, choose, experience, change your mind and learn what matters to you.

Someone else’s certainty does not make your uncertainty a failure. You see other people from the outside and yourself from the inside. Their confidence cannot tell you whether you are behind.

You are allowed to question who you are. You don’t owe an old version of yourself permanence. Ask what still feels like you, what no longer does, and what you might be becoming.

Who you are is allowed to change as you do. Growth will sometimes make old beliefs, relationships, dreams and versions of yourself stop fitting. That does not automatically mean you’ve lost yourself.

You are not required to find one final version of yourself. Know yourself enough to choose intentionally, but leave room to evolve. A strong sense of self can become clearer and more coherent over time without becoming permanently fixed.
